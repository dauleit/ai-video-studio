Privacy Policy – AI Video Studio

Last updated: 12 November 2026

AI Video Studio is a personal application used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API for the owner's channels: uploading videos, setting thumbnails, scheduling publication, and reading channel and video statistics.

Storage

OAuth tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any third party, sold, or shared.

Other users

The app does not collect personal data from anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
